# 60 — The Workshop's screens on the Control Room (WP71)

> **Status:** design of record for WP71 (Phase P; `42-DAY4-ROADMAP.md` §3, `41-TARGET-DESIGN-V4.md` §6.12 stages C–D; retires G32). Written 2026-09-06 on branch `wp71-control-room-v2`, before stage A. WP57 (`44-CONTROL-ROOM.md`) built the system — the tokens, the grammar, the instruments, the Boundary — and left every existing screen as it was (`44-…` §7); this WP re-cuts them on it and finishes the visual-regression pass `44-…` §8 deferred.

## 1. Purpose

A practitioner reads the Workshop the way an engineer reads a control room: every number on an instrument, every rate against a gate, every series on a tape, every verdict a lamp with a word beside it. WP57 made the instruments and drew the Boundary with them; the older screens still show stat tiles, emoji verdicts, hand-set `width:` bars and a table whose cells set their own fill. This WP puts every Workshop screen on the one system so a reader learns the grammar once, makes the "no chart outside the grammar" rule true in fact rather than only in lint, and commits the screenshots of every Workshop route so a screen cannot change shape unnoticed — with the Kit's screenshots in the same set, unchanged.

## 2. Where the code actually is — and what the contract test found

Read before writing: `44-CONTROL-ROOM.md` §2, §4.4, §7, §8 and its stage notes; `lib/control-room/dataviz.ts`; every component under `components/control-room/`; every `routes/workshop/**/+page.svelte`; `eslint.config.js`'s chart rule; `e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `e2e/a11y.spec.ts`, `playwright.config.ts`; the e2e specs that read the marks being replaced (`campaigns`, `evaluators`, `sinks`, `telemetry`, `evals`).

1. **No Workshop screen draws an `<svg>` or a `<canvas>`** — the lint rule holds already. The charts drawn by hand are the ones `44-…` §2 named: the Eval Matrix's `success-grid` (a table of `--fill` buttons over `rampStep`), Telemetry's guardrail mix (a `width:` percent span), the Bench Dashboard's `.tile` readouts and campaign tiles, and a dozen chips (`.chip[data-outcome]`, `.verdict`, `✅ pass` / `❌ fail` / `⚪ inconclusive` text) across Campaigns, Evaluators, Incidents, Compare, the Audit Centre, the Test Bench and the safety case. **Fixed (stage A):** each becomes the instrument the grammar has for it — `Matrix`, `Tape`, `Readout`/`Strip`, `Lamp` — and `rampStep` in `eval-cells.ts` loses its last caller.
2. **The instruments already carry what the screens need.** `Matrix` cells take `{ value, label, note? }` and a click, with `${testId}-${row}-${col}` on every cell; `Tape` takes series in lane colours with flags and a `compact` sparkline form; `Meter` takes a rate against a gate with a `direction`; `Readout` a label, a value, a unit and a delta; `Lamp` a `Status` (`pass` | `fail` | `inconclusive` | `live`) with a label. **Reused unchanged:** no instrument gains a prop for this WP; a screen that needs a shape the grammar lacks is a finding, and none did.
3. **The e2e reads the marks by text.** `campaigns.spec.ts` expects `✅ pass`, `❌ fail`, `✅ PASSED`; `evaluators.spec.ts` reads `data-verdict`; `sinks.spec.ts` reads `failed 0` and a status code from the sink's status line; `evals.spec.ts` clicks `square-<card>-<brain>` and reads `100%`. **Decided:** a `Lamp`'s word is the verdict (`pass`, `fail`, `inconclusive`, `PASSED`), so the specs drop the emoji and keep the word; the data attributes stay on the rows; the sink's status line stays as text beside a lamp (a reader greps it); the Eval Matrix's cells keep their ids through `Matrix`'s `testId` — `square-<card>-<brain>` is what `Matrix` writes when its `testId` is `square`.
4. **Explain lives in the inspector.** `41-…` §6.12 puts "Explain and Chain beside the Boundary pane"; WP66 drew Explain inside the inspector behind a checkbox, and WP65 drew the Chain there too. **Fixed:** the Boundary region becomes two columns — the map, and beside it the Explain panel (when asked for) and the Chain for the selected row — so the picture and its explanation sit together; the inspector keeps the diff and the raw JSON. Every test id keeps its name.
5. **Screenshots exist for one platform only.** `e2e/__screenshots__/win32/` holds six baselines; the CI runner is Linux, and Playwright refuses to compare across platforms. **Decided (stage B):** the `visual` project runs in CI in its own job; its Linux baselines are generated by the job itself on a first run (`--update-snapshots`, the folder uploaded as an artefact) and committed alongside the Windows ones — a baseline per platform, as `playwright.config.ts`'s `snapshotPathTemplate` always allowed. From then on the job diffs.
6. **Screenshots read the clock and the locale.** The Run Browser and the dashboard print `toLocaleString()` dates; a shot taken on a runner in another zone differs by a column. **Fixed:** the `visual` project pins `locale: 'en-GB'` and `timezoneId: 'UTC'`, and the fixture corpus it seeds carries fixed timestamps (the golden traces), so the same screen shoots the same on every machine.
7. **Axe covers five Workshop routes.** `a11y.spec.ts` audits the Run Browser and the Run Lab through the Desk's test; the other Workshop routes have never been audited. **Fixed (stage B):** `a11y-workshop.spec.ts` runs axe over every Workshop route on the same fixture corpus, in the default project.
8. **Telemetry has no series drawn at all.** The day buckets `telemetrySeries` folds (runs, success rate, the domain series, the drift flags) are printed as a list of flags with no axis. **Fixed:** a `Tape` of the day axis — runs and success rate per day, every domain series in its lane — with the drift flags on the series they flag; the flag list stays beneath it as the reader's text.

## 3. Principles

- **One grammar, every screen.** A number is a `Readout`, a header a `Strip`, a verdict a `Lamp`, a rate against a gate a `Meter`, a grid of rates a `Matrix`, a series a `Tape`. Nothing else draws.
- **The word beside the mark.** Every lamp says its status; every matrix cell holds its value; every meter engraves its number. Never colour alone (`04-…` §7).
- **Geometry stays where a test id lives.** A screen keeps its test ids and its e2e; where a mark's *text* changes (emoji to a word) the spec changes in the same commit and says why.
- **The Kit does not move.** No Kit component changes; the Kit's screenshots are in the diff to prove it.
- **Screenshots are evidence, not decoration.** Every Workshop route on a fixed corpus, per platform, diffed in CI; a deliberate change re-baselines with a dated note.

## 5. UX

The Workshop reads the same, on instruments: a verdict is a lamp with a word, a rate a needle against a mark, a grid of rates a matrix whose darker cells are more successful (teal, as the grammar has it), a series a tape with its flags. Toy names stay in the Kit; the Workshop's words are the instruments' (`readout`, `lamp`, `meter`, `tape`, `matrix`).

## 6. Determinism

A screen renders from records, summaries and reports; the folds are the ones the screens already call (`telemetryFrom`, `telemetrySeries`, `summariseCampaign`, `matrixSize`). The screenshots pin the clock, the locale, the viewport and reduced motion, and seed from fixtures with fixed timestamps.

## 7. Non-goals

The art (textures, the instrument icons — WP73); the Guards screen's prose results as lamps (a fixture result is a sentence); a Meter for every percentage in every table (a meter is for a rate *against a gate*); re-cutting the Kit; screenshots of the Play routes mid-run beyond the Desk shot that exists.

## 9. Risks

- **A shot flakes on a runner** (a font, a scrollbar). The `visual` job runs at the same viewport and reduced motion as locally, on Chromium only; a flake is a re-run, and a real drift a diff.
- **A screen's text changes and the e2e's word with it.** The specs read the lamp's word; `Lamp`'s labels are fixed in the grammar.
- **Re-baselining hides a regression.** A baseline changes only with a dated note in this file naming the screen.
