# The domain pack blueprint

> **Status:** written 2026-09-13 (WP108, `docs/design-day2/84-DAY6-ROADMAP.md`; `83-TARGET-DESIGN-V6.md` §6.6.3, decision D13, tenet 31). The checklist as prose. Every item names the check that refuses a gap (`checkDomainPack` in `@craftabot/pack-testkit`, `93-DOMAIN-PACK.md` §3) and the bank's own file as the worked example. Three notes apply it without writing code: [`HEALTHCARE.md`](HEALTHCARE.md), [`LOGISTICS.md`](LOGISTICS.md), [`MANUFACTURING.md`](MANUFACTURING.md). A test holds this document to its own rule — every checklist row names a `check` and a bank file (`packages/harness/src/blueprints.test.ts`).

A **domain** is a manifest of manifests: one world pack, one journey pack per journey, and a `DomainSpec` that says which packs are the domain's, which decision kinds are whose at what autonomy level and by what source, which classes are special category, which journeys ship and which are out and why. Craft A Bot's instruments — desks, journeys, books, campaigns, the assurance pack, the Monitor, the Journey Canvas — run over any domain that meets the checklist, and the checklist is code. The bank (`packages/packs/fs-bank` and its seven desks) passed it first (`93-…` §6 records what it found). `craftabot scaffold domain` types the shape out (`93-…` §4; the manual's §52 is the how-to).

## 1. What the pieces are

**A world** is a pack with a root entity in the domain's own word, generated from a seed through the synthetic primitives in `@craftabot/desk` (never a real record — hard rule 9), with the truth beside it: what the assistant may be shown, what a look-up may earn, and what only the evaluators read. The bank's is `fs-bank/src/model.ts` (the customer), `generate/` (the generators over the calibration table), `records.ts` (the split into revealed, hidden and truth, a classification on every record).

**A journey** is a workflow: the stages a case passes through, each with an executor — a rule, an agent on a card synthesised for the stage, a person, a service line — and its configurations by autonomy level, `rules-only` at one end and the assistant everywhere at the other. Its book draws cases from the seed with the rule's verdict in truth; its golden run is `rules-only` agreeing with the rule; its red run is the adversary failing the card the desk ships. The bank's: `fs-lending/src/workflow.ts` (five configurations, the ceilings cited), `book.ts`, `journey.test.ts`.

**Truth** is what the world knows and the assistant cannot read: the rule's verdict, the label, the cohort. It leaves the world only on `run.finished` and only the evaluators read it (`45-TRUTH-SYNTHETIC.md`). `checkDesk` refuses a truth leaf that leaks into a snapshot. The bank's: `DeskTruth` on every desk's `world/cases.ts`.

**A line** is a system the journey reaches through the Connector — the record, the ledger, the schedule — declared as content with a risk tier on every operation (_observe_, _reversible_, _irreversible_), answering from the world's state in `simulate`, from a cassette, or live under declared egress (`47-SERVICE-LINES.md`). The bank's ten: `fs-bank/src/lines/*.ts`.

**An obligation tag** names a rule the domain is held to — a regulator's, a statute's, a guidance's — with a gloss, so a journey's stage, a card, an evaluator and a control row can all say which rule they serve and a report can group by it. The bank's vocabulary: `fs-bank/src/obligations.ts`.

**A control-map row** is a claim of _relevance_, never of compliance: this obligation, met by this card and shown by this evaluator, `unreviewed` until a compliance reader has read it (`53-ASSURANCE-PACK.md`). The bank's: `fs-bank/src/controls/` and each desk's `controls/rows.ts`.

**A ceiling** is the highest autonomy level a decision kind may run at — measured by the human-load rows, never enforced by the runtime (`64-…` §6.5) — with a source. Every kind a workflow's `decisionKindOf` returns is a decision right of the domain at that level. The bank's: `fs-bank/src/domain.ts` (twenty-five rights) and each desk's `decision-rights.ts`.

**A calibration row** carries a distribution, a source (a publication with a URL and the date read, or a stated assumption with a note saying what was simplified), a tolerance, and a review state (`66-CALIBRATION.md`). The generators draw from rows, and a test over a population holds the draw to the row within tolerance. The bank's: `fs-bank/src/calibration/`.

**The conformance kit refuses**: a desk whose truth leaks (`checkDesk`), a line without tiers (`checkServiceLine`), an evaluator reading truth it did not declare (`checkEvaluator`), a row citing evidence no pack ships (`checkControlMap`), a calibration row that neither cites nor states (`checkCalibration`), a fixture with the shape a real identifier has (`checkSynthetic`), and — over the lot — a domain that does not meet its own spec (`checkDomainPack`).

**A domain reaches the shelf and an edition** by registration, not by mechanism: the packs under `packages/packs/`, listed in the harness's default packs and the Workshop's plan chain, put in an edition's box (`59-EDITIONS.md`), a Playground page per desk. `95-FS-ONBOARDING.md` §1 lists every seam one desk registers on; a domain repeats it per journey.

## 2. The checklist

Each row: the item, the check that refuses it, the bank's file that meets it. `checkDomainPack(spec, registry, { manifests, personas })` returns the items unmet, or nothing.

| #   | Item                                                                                                                                                                                                    | `check`                                          | The bank's file                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0   | The spec validates: `schemaVersion: 1`, a qualified id, the packs, the vocabulary, the rights, the table, the ontology, the matrix, the personas, the glossary (`docs/schemas/domain.schema.json`).     | `domain.schema`                                  | `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/domain.ts`                                                                                           |
| 1   | Every pack the spec names is installed: the world and every journey.                                                                                                                                    | `domain.packs-registered`                        | `packages/harness/src/config.ts` (`defaultPacks`)                                                                                |
| 2   | Every `shipped` journey is a registered workflow; every workflow a journey pack registers is on the matrix; a `supporting` journey is a workflow or a world, or says why; every `out` journey says why. | `domain.journey-ships`, `domain.journey-out-why` | `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/domain.ts` (`journeys`: eight shipped, one supporting, four out)                                     |
| 3   | Every obligation a journey carries — the workflow's and each stage's — is in the vocabulary.                                                                                                            | `domain.journey-obligations`                     | `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/obligations.ts`; `packages/packs/fs-fraud/src/workflow.ts` (`obligations`)                           |

| 6   | The calibration table the spec names is on an installed manifest, validates, and every row cites a publication or states an assumption with a note.                                                     | `domain.calibration`                             | `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/calibration/`                                                                                        |
| 7   | Every special-category class is an ontology class, and every special-category record a desk holds is of a class the spec names as such.                                                                 | `domain.special-category`                        | `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/ontology.ts` (`Vulnerability`); `packages/packs/fs-bank/src/records.ts` (the `vulnerability` record) |

| 10  | Every journey pack that ships a journey ships a book on the workflow and a campaign on the manifest.                                                                                                    | `domain.journey-evidence`                        | `packages/packs/fs-collections/src/book.ts`, `campaign.ts`                                                                       |

**What the check cannot see from a manifest** is the pack's own tests, and the blueprint asks for them by name: the **golden run** (`rules-only` and Level 4 to the same decision, byte-stable — `packages/packs/fs-lending/src/journey.test.ts`), the **red run** (the adversary failing the card unguarded and passing it under the card — `packages/packs/fs-advice/src/campaign.test.ts`), at least one **matched pair** where a cohort could be treated differently (`packages/packs/fs-lending/src/world/cases.ts`, `matched-pair`, and the parity gate on `campaigns/fs-lending-baseline.json`), and the **synthetic sweep** over every fixture (`packages/desk/src/synthetic-sweep.test.ts`, which walks `packages`, `campaigns` and `examples`). A **calibration review** is a gate of its own: `checkCalibration(table, { requireReview: true })` fails a row a reader has not read against its source, and a domain that claims its table reviewed adds that call to its tests.

## 3. How to use a note

Each of the three notes applies the checklist to an industry without writing code: the world, the journeys, the obligations and the rights, the special category, what the blueprint predicts is hard, a checkbox per checklist item reviewed against the scaffold's tree (`93-…` §4), **the list of what would be typed by count**, and **a sizing** in the roadmap's units (`84-…` §2: **S** a session or two, **M** several, **L** a week). Each carries a `DomainSpec` fixture (`fixtures/<name>.domain.json`) that validates the schema and fails `checkDomainPack` for the one reason a note can — no packs are installed — which is the test that the check checks (`packages/harness/src/blueprints.test.ts`). None is scheduled: a decision to build one is a decision about a number.
