# Governance mapping — what ships, in the frameworks' own words

> **Posture** (`docs/design-day2/08-GOVERNANCE-GUARDRAILS.md` §6): alignment notes, not certification. Craft A Bot's simulator controls nothing real, which is precisely its value as a rehearsal space — so nothing here claims compliance with anything. Each row says what a shipped mechanism _is_, where it lives, and which clauses it can be _described_ under, so that a person building the real thing knows which conversation each piece belongs in. Written for WP50 (`38-GOVERNANCE-1-0.md` §4.3); the control numbers are `19-AI-SAFETY-GOVERNANCE-REFERENCE.md` §9's, and the WP that shipped each is in `18-DAY2-ROADMAP.md` §7 or `27-DAY3-ROADMAP.md` §8.

**How to read a row.** _Control_ is the shortlist entry. _Where_ is the export or screen — `governance` means `@craftabot/governance`, `governance/reports` its reports subpath, `core` the engine contracts, `Workshop` a screen in the app, `harness` the headless host. _Describable under_ lists, in order: NIST AI RMF 1.0 (function and subcategory), the EU AI Act (article, for a high-risk system's provider — the regime whose vocabulary these mechanisms borrow), ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (clause), and OWASP's Top 10 for Agentic Applications (ASI) or LLM Applications (LLM) where a threat is what the control answers. A blank cell means no clause fits honestly.
